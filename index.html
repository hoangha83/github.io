About

I am currently a lecturer and researcher in probability and statistics at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Science in Ho Chi Minh City. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Laboratoire de Mathématiques Raphaël Salem (LMRS), University of Rouen Normandie (2018–2020), and before that a temporary lecturer and research assistant (ATER) at the Laboratoire de Mathématiques d'Orsay, University of Paris-Sud 11 (2017–2018).

I obtained my Ph.D. in statistics on 28 November 2016 at the University of Lille 1, France, under the supervision of Viet Chi Tran (University Gustave Eiffel), Thanh Mai Pham Ngoc (University Paris-Sud) and Vincent Rivoirard (University Paris Dauphine).

Research Interests

Nonparametric estimation Inverse problems Mixture models & functional data analysis Stochastic models for population dynamics
